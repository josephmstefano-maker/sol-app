SOL

A private personal assistant.

SOL is personal assistant software that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service: it has a single user, its owner, and nobody else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission, SOL connects to the owner's own Google account to help organize email: it suggests how to sort incoming mail, and it applies labels or archives messages only after the owner approves each change.

SOL is not sold, has no other users, and shows no advertising.
